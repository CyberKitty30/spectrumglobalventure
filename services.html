FTWZ

Free Trade & Warehousing Zones

The Free Trade and Warehousing Zones (FTWZ) is a special category of Special Economic Zone governed by the SEZ Act and Rules. We help you leverage FTWZ for storage, value addition, re-export, and deferred duty benefits.

  • Bonded warehousing
  • Value-added services (labeling, packing, kitting)
  • Re-export facilitation
  • Duty deferment advantages
  • Inventory management support
